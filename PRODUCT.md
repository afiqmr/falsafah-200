# Product

## Platform

Web app, installed to the phone's home screen. Most students use a phone; some use a laptop or
iPad. It must work on old phones and on mobile data.

## Users

- **Students** of the Advanced Diploma in Al-Quranic Philosophy at Institut Falsafah Al-Quran,
  Year 1 and Year 2. Adults of all ages, many not technical, studying around work and family.
- **The lecturer and class admin**, who follow class progress and share Dr. HALO-N's recordings.

A first-time student with no training must be able to register and start Kaji in under a minute.

## Purpose

Help students understand and memorise the 200 falsafah of _Al-Fathun Nawa Jilid 7_ for their
exams: the ayat, its maksud, the author's Kata Kunci and Catitan, and how each falsafah differs
from others with a similar theme.

Success means a student studies a little every day, knows which falsafah are weak, and can recite
and explain each one without looking.

## Principles

- **Faithful to the book.** Titles, maksud, Kata Kunci, Catitan and page numbers come from Jilid 7.
- **Recall before reveal.** Kaji asks the student to remember first, then shows the answer.
- **Honest scoring.** Gred comes from recent answers only, and the student marks Ingat or Belum
  ingat honestly after Dedah.
- **Small daily habit.** Targets are small (Kaji 10, Kuiz 3) and a streak rewards coming back.
- **Bahasa Melayu first.** Labels and messages are in BM. A few familiar English terms stay, such
  as Word by word and Share.
- **Private by default.** Progress belongs to the student. Recordings stay with registered students.

## Not in scope

- Tajwid or makhraj assessment.
- Content from other Jilid, except the exam notes in Nota.
- Public leaderboards or comparing students by name.
